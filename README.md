# 📊 E-commerce Sales Analysis

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to uncover actionable business insights related to revenue growth, customer behavior, product performance, discount effectiveness, payment preferences, and regional trends.

The analysis was performed using **MySQL** for data querying and **Power BI** for dashboard visualization.

# 🎯 Business Objective

The goal of this project is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that can help decision-makers:

* Improve revenue growth
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Optimize discount strategies
* Identify high-performing products and regions
* Enhance customer retention
* Support data-driven decision making

# 📌 Business Questions Solved

## 📈 Sales & Revenue Analysis

* What is total revenue over time?
* What are the top 10 products by revenue?
* What is the average order value (AOV)?
* Are there seasonal trends in orders and revenue?
* What is the trend of discount vs revenue over time?

---

## 👥 Customer Analysis

* Which customer segments spend the most?
* What is the repeat purchase rate?
* What is the customer lifetime value (CLV) distribution?
* How does signup date correlate with spending behavior?

---

## 🛍 Product & Pricing Analysis

* Which categories have the highest demand?
* What is the price vs quantity relationship?
* Which products are frequently discounted?
* Do higher discounts actually increase sales volume?

---

## 💳 Payment & Channel Analysis

* Which payment methods are most used?
* Does payment method impact order value?
* Which channel + payment combinations drive the most revenue?

---

## 🌍 Regional Performance Analysis

* Which regions show growing or declining performance?

---

# 🔍 Key Insights Generated

## 📈 Revenue Insights

* Revenue showed an overall upward trend with periodic fluctuations.
* Online sales channels generated the highest contribution to revenue.
* Seasonal patterns were observed in monthly sales performance.

---

## 👥 Customer Insights

* Customers aged **26–35** contributed the highest spending.
* A small group of high-value customers generated a major portion of revenue.
* Repeat purchase rate indicated moderate customer retention.

---

## 🛍 Product Insights

* Certain product categories dominated overall demand.
* Top-performing products contributed significantly to total revenue.
* Product demand varied based on pricing levels.

---

## 💸 Discount Insights

* Higher discounts did not significantly increase sales volume.
* Customers showed relatively weak price sensitivity.
* Excessive discounting may reduce profitability without major volume growth.

---

## 💳 Payment Insights

* Digital payment methods were the most frequently used.
* Credit card transactions showed higher average order value.
* Payment behavior varied across sales channels.

---

## 🌍 Regional Insights

* Some regions demonstrated strong revenue growth.
* Other regions showed declining or stagnant performance.
